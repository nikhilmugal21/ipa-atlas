Attributions & limitations

The official IPA chart defines the basic symbols. PHOIBLE documents phonological inventories; the CMU dictionary supplies selected US English lexical pronunciations; and the IPA Handbook has language illustrations and recordings under separate permissions.

These sources are complementary, not interchangeable: a phoneme does not identify one exact acoustic realisation.
